---
name: Clinician prototype scope
description: Why every clinician surface must visibly identify itself as a proof-of-concept.
---

Clinician-facing surfaces must retain a persistent, non-dismissible “Proof-of-concept” label, including public clinician pages, authentication, profile setup/editing, care-portal journals and phone clinician roles. Patient-facing pages and the selected patient sign-in role must not show it.

**Why:** The user explicitly designated the clinician tools as an early proof-of-concept: clinician identities are not verified and features may change. The warning must remain visible as clinician routes are added, not just on the landing page.

**How to apply:** On web clinician surfaces, keep navigation formatting unchanged and place the shared full-width proof-of-concept banner immediately below navigation, not beside the wordmark. The user requested this placement because the inline badge disrupted the header. Phone clinician surfaces retain their platform badge. Reuse shared care-team copy. Do not remove the label merely because a particular clinician feature becomes functional.
