How it works

Two people, one recovery.

Follow a sample patient and her sports medicine physician through Stillwell. They're made up, and so is their data. The screens are the real ones.

Patient · college soccer player, 20

Maya took a head-to-head hit in a match on Saturday. By day 3 she has headaches most of the day, screens wear her out, and she doesn't know what's safe. She wants to get back on the field, safely.

  1. 1Tells Stillwell her story, in her own words
  2. 2Sees where she might be on the recovery roadmap
  3. 3Checks in each day and shares her journal with Ray
